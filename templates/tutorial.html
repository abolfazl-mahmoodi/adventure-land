The First Scroll: How to Survive

Learn how quests, heroes, locations, and difficulty work.

Welcome, Adventurer.

Before entering the world of Vaelthorn, Eldoria, and the Legacy Dungeons, you must learn how each quest works. Every adventure requires a balanced party, the right strategy, and the courage to survive unknown dangers.

1. Every Quest Needs a Core Party

Every quest begins with the Core Party. Your Warrior protects the team, your Mage attacks with magic, and your Healer keeps the party alive. Without these three heroes, no adventure can begin.

Warrior

Protects the party on the front line.

Mage

Attacks with magic from a distance.

Healer

Restores health and keeps everyone alive.

2. Quest Types Unlock Special Heroes

Every quest type unlocks one selectable specialist role with one place. Stealth missions gain an Assassin, combat missions a Time-Lost Fighter, exploration missions a Future Reader, and puzzle missions a Rune Solver.

4. Choose Your Difficulty

Choose your difficulty carefully. Easy quests help you learn the world, medium quests test your teamwork, and hard quests punish every mistake but offer the greatest rewards.

5. Understand Quest Time

Every quest lists its duration in minutes on its quest card. This is how long your party will be occupied once a session begins — plan your other quest sessions around it, since you can't join two sessions that overlap in time.
