Guild Rules

The Tutorial explains how to play. This page lists the actual constraints the guild enforces — what you can and can't do, and why a button might be disabled.

I

Party Roles

Every quest session has a fixed number of places per role:

    • {% endfor %}
II

Reserving a Place

You may reserve 1 or {{ max_places }} places on a single participation. The second place is for a named companion who chooses their own available role. Your role and the companion's role each consume one place from the corresponding capacity.

III

Weekly Limit

Each adventurer can join at most {{ max_sessions }} quest sessions during the fictional week. You also can't join two sessions that overlap in time.

IV

Changing Your Mind

A participation can be edited or cancelled only if its session starts more than {{ modify_cutoff_hours }} hours after the guild's current simulated time. After that, it's locked in.

V

Joining a Session

You can't join a quest session that has already taken place, relative to the guild's current simulated time.

VI

Quests vs. Sessions

Once a quest is created, its story, type, and difficulty can never change — only its sessions (day, time, location) can be scheduled or edited, and only while a session still has no participants.

VII

One Location, One Session

The guild never double-books a location — two sessions can't overlap in time at the same location.

VIII

The Guild Master

The Guild Master creates quests and schedules sessions, but never joins a party — their role is to run the board, not adventure on it.
